## 🔴 CRITICAL — Data contradicts itself across pages

The same route shows different distance, duration and price depending on which page you land on. This destroys trust, and it also poisons your structured data — Google and LLMs see conflicting facts about your own service and stop trusting the entity.

| Route | Homepage says | /routes says | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jeddah Airport → Makkah | 85 km, 1h 15m | 80 km, 1h 00m | distance + duration mismatch |
| Riyadh → Dammam | 400 km, 4h 00m | 390 km, 4h 30m | distance + duration mismatch |
| Makkah → Madinah | 450 km | 430 km | distance mismatch |
| Madinah → Jeddah Airport | SAR 549 (Umrah block) | SAR 380 (routes grid) | **44% price gap on the same page-set** |
| Jeddah → Madinah | SAR 549 | Madinah → Jeddah = SAR 350 | reverse direction 36% cheaper, unexplained |
| Route count | "56+ Routes Covered" | "50+ taxi routes" (meta) / ~70 actually listed | three different numbers |

**Root cause:** there is no single source of truth for route data. Prices/distances are hardcoded in multiple components.

## 🔴 CRITICAL — Broken duration calculations

Several durations look auto-generated by a buggy formula:

- Riyadh Airport → Riyadh City: 35 km in **1h 30m** (real: ~35 min)
- Jeddah Airport → Jeddah City: 20 km in **1h 30m** (real: ~25 min)
- AlUla Airport → AlUla Resorts: 30 km in **1h 30m**
- Abha Airport → Soudah: 45 km in **1h 50m**
- Riyadh Airport → KAFD: 40 km in **1h 35m**

A traveller comparing you to Uber sees "1.5 hours for 20 km" and leaves.

## 🔴 CRITICAL — Review schema risk

Reviews are labelled *"Google Review — Verified Trip"* but there is no link to a Google Business Profile, no review count, no dates. If these are marked up as `Review`/`AggregateRating` schema without a real verifiable source, that is a **manual action risk** under Google's spam policies — and it is the single fastest way to lose all rankings. Either connect real GBP reviews or remove the schema and the "Google Review" label.

## 🔴 CONVERSION KILLER — "confirms within 1–2 hours"

Stated twice on the homepage. For airport transfers and Umrah transfers people book on impulse, often from an airport lounge. A 1–2 hour wait for a price = they book Careem/Uber/a competitor. There is a price calculator but **no instant confirmed booking, no online payment, no confirmation number in real time**. This alone can explain most of your missing enquiries.

## 🟠 HIGH — English only

`og:locale` is `en_SA`, there is a `🇬🇧 en` switcher but no visible `ar`, `ur`, `id`, `tr`, `ms`, `fr` versions and no `hreflang` cluster. You are invisible to:

- **Arabic** searchers (the largest in-Kingdom volume, and the highest-paying corporate/VIP segment)
- **Indonesian / Malay** Umrah traffic (huge volume, group bookings, low competition in English SERPs)
- **Urdu / Turkish / French** pilgrim traffic

## 🟠 HIGH — Structural gaps in money pages

- No **city × service** matrix pages (e.g. `/jeddah/airport-transfer`, `/riyadh/corporate-chauffeur`) — only `/locations/x` and `/services/y` separately.
- Hotel-level pages exist **only** for Jeddah Airport → Makkah hotels. Madinah, Riyadh, Khobar hotel pages are missing entirely.
- No **vehicle × route** pricing pages (GMC Yukon Jeddah–Makkah price, Hiace Umrah group price).
- No **B2B landing pages** (hotels, travel agencies, Umrah operators, corporate accounts) with a proper lead form — only a generic `/partners`.
- No comparison content ("Taxi vs Uber vs Careem Jeddah to Makkah price"), which is one of the highest-intent, lowest-competition query classes in this niche.

## 🟠 HIGH — Zero AI-answer optimisation

No `llms.txt`, no answer-first content blocks, no explicit fare tables in machine-readable form, no entity disambiguation, no author/organisation E-E-A-T signals (no CR number, no Saudi physical address, no Ministry of Transport licence number displayed as verifiable text). ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and AI Overviews cannot cite you confidently.

## 🟡 MEDIUM

- Footer: *"Created with absolute elegance"* and `© 2026` — reads as template filler; replace with legal entity name, CR number, VAT number, address.
- SAR-only pricing; no USD/GBP/EUR/IDR display for international bookers.
- Only 3 guides published — nowhere near enough for topical authority.
- Filter dropdown on `/routes` is broken: options show truncated tokens like `Al`, `Doha,`, `Manama,`, `Abu` — a string-splitting bug.
- No trust page for licences, insurance, driver vetting.
- Testimonial names + flags look synthetic; add real verifiable social proof.
